# LOKVAANI AI

### Preserve the Past. Understand It. Build the Future

LOKVAANI AI is an **AI-powered indigenous knowledge and cultural heritage platform** that preserves disappearing traditional skills, knowledge, practices and local products while giving communities control over how their knowledge is shared.

The initial focus is **Uttarakhand**, with a vision to expand across India.

---

## The Problem

Traditional knowledge such as:

* Farming techniques
* Handicrafts
* Food preservation
* Plant knowledge
* Folk traditions
* Traditional architecture
* Local tools and skills
* Oral histories

is often passed through generations rather than documented digitally.

As knowledge holders disappear, valuable knowledge can disappear with them.

At the same time, younger generations, researchers and organizations have difficulty discovering authentic local knowledge.

---

## Our Solution

LOKVAANI AI allows people to **record traditional knowledge through video, audio, images or documents**.

AI automatically converts the content into structured, searchable knowledge.

### AI can generate:

* Transcriptions
* Translations
* Step-by-step procedures
* Required materials
* Knowledge categories
* Video chapters
* Summaries
* Searchable metadata
* Related knowledge
* Preservation-risk scores

This reduces the manual effort required to document cultural knowledge.

---

## Uttarakhand Knowledge Map

The platform starts with the **13 districts of Uttarakhand**.

Users can explore:

**District → Community → Category → Tradition → Knowledge Holder**

Categories can include:

* Traditional Agriculture
* Food Heritage
* Plant Knowledge
* Handicrafts
* Folk Culture
* Traditional Architecture
* Water Practices
* Oral History
* Vanishing Skills
* Local Products

---

## Community Ownership & Access

Not all knowledge should be public.

LOKVAANI AI provides access levels such as:

**Public | Registered | Community Only | Research | Restricted**

Each knowledge item can have a unique **Knowledge ID** containing information about its source, contributor, ownership, access permissions and verification status.

The goal is:

> **Preserve knowledge without taking control away from the community.**

---

## AI-Powered Knowledge Discovery

Users can ask natural-language questions such as:

> "What traditional food preservation methods are used in Tehri?"

AI searches the knowledge base and provides relevant practices, people, locations, videos and related information.

The platform can also connect traditional knowledge with **scientific research**, while clearly distinguishing traditional claims from scientifically established evidence.

---

## Knowledge at Risk

AI can identify traditions that may be disappearing based on factors such as:

* Number of practitioners
* Younger generation participation
* Documentation level
* Frequency of practice
* Geographic concentration

Example:

**Traditional Craft — Risk: 89/100 — HIGH**

This can help NGOs, universities and government organizations decide what knowledge needs preservation attention.
