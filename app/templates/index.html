How it works

From a recording on a phone to a verified record anyone can search.

  1. Share

    Upload an audio or video recording, or write your story. Choose whether it may be public.

  2. AI assists

    Speech is transcribed and translated where a provider is configured. Nothing is made up.

  3. You check

    You correct the transcript and tags, then submit the record for verification.

  4. Stewards verify

    A Community Steward listens, edits, and approves, rejects or asks for corrections.

  5. Everyone learns

    Approved public records become searchable and are cited when you ask LOKVAANI AI.

Ask the archive a question

Answers come only from approved records, with the source shown. If the archive does not know, it says so.
