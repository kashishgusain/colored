About LOKVAANI AI

Lok means people. Vaani means voice. The platform exists so the voices of Uttarakhand's elders are not lost.

What it does

Contributors share recordings and stories. AI helps with transcription, translation, summaries and tags. A Community Steward verifies every record. Approved public records become searchable and are the only source the Ask AI assistant may use.

Who is in charge

AI assists the community and never takes authority from it. A record is never published automatically, and AI never marks anything as verified.

Honest limits

  • Speech models do not officially support Garhwali or Kumaoni. Transcripts of these languages are rough drafts and need a fluent speaker.
  • Translations are machine-made until a person edits them, and are labelled so.
  • The assistant answers only from approved records, can still misread them, and always shows its sources.
  • If no AI provider is configured, the site says so and falls back to search and excerpts.

Launch region

The first launch covers the thirteen districts of Uttarakhand.
