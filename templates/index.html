The problem

Some knowledge disappears without ever being written down.

A farming method, a seed-preservation trick, a weaving pattern, a way of managing water — these often exist only in one person's memory. When that person is gone, so is the knowledge, unless someone records it in time.

How it works

Record → Understand → Verify → Preserve → Discover

  1. Record

    A knowledge holder records themselves speaking naturally, in their own language.

  2. Understand

    AI transcribes, translates, and organizes the recording into structured knowledge.

  3. Verify

    The knowledge holder reviews the AI's work, then a Community Steward confirms it.

  4. Preserve

    The record gets a permanent Knowledge ID and enters the searchable archive.

  5. Discover

    Anyone with permission can find it through search or by asking LOKVAANI AI directly.
